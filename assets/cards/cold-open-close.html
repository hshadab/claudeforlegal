Log vs. Receipt

Log

Written after the fact
  • The operator’s own account
  • Produced after the action ran
  • Trusted on faith

Receipt

Generated before the action
  • A cryptographic proof of the check
  • Verifiable by anyone — no key, no login
  • Proves the verdict without revealing the rules
