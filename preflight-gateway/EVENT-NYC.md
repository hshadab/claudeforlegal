## Listing copy

Guardrails Live: Watch an AI Agent Get Blocked

Claude can review a contract. It can also send, file, and sign. That last part is
where the risk lives.

This is a live demonstration, not a talk. On stage, the same prompt runs twice:
without a guardrail, Claude mistakenly emails a privileged document to opposing
counsel. With Preflight from ICME Labs connected, a mathematical process applies the
firm's rule and blocks the send before it happens. Every decision leaves a
cryptographic receipt anyone in the room can verify from their phone, with no key and
no login.

Then it's your turn. Bring a rule from your firm or company, written in plain English.
We will translate it into formal logic and enforce it on a live agent while you watch.

Who this is for: lawyers and legal ops leaders putting AI to work, founders and
engineers building agents, and anyone who thinks "the model will probably behave" is
not a compliance strategy.
