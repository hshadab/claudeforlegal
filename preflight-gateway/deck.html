What you're about to see
Claude reviews a contract, then proposes what to do with it. Before any action runs, Preflight checks it against your rules and either lets it through or stops it. Here are the four moments.
1
Claude for Legal does the real work.
It reviews a vendor draft, flags the problems, and writes the memo.
2
A mistake tries to send a privileged draft to the other side. Blocked.
The fail-safe checks the action and stops it before it sends. A human or AI slip, caught every time.
3
An approved NDA goes to signature. Allowed.
The same fail-safe lets a clean action through. It stops mistakes, not work.
4
Every check leaves a receipt.
Anyone can verify it in under a second, with no key and no login.
How it works
Every check, allowed or blocked, leaves a receipt anyone can verify in under a second.
The rules it checks
Plain English the firm writes once, on the left. The same rule as formal logic, on the right. A solver checks the logic the same way every time.
BLOCK
A privileged document sent outside the firm domain.
privileged ∧ recipient ∉ firm → block
BLOCK
A signature or filing no reviewer approved.
sign ∧ ¬approved → block
BLOCK
Document contents sent to an unapproved domain.
send ∧ domain ∉ firm → block
BLOCK
A change to credentials or auth files.
modify(credentials) → block
PERMIT
Reading a local document that is never sent out.
readLocal ∧ ¬send → permit
∧ and  ·  ¬ not  ·  → then  ·  ∉ outside
